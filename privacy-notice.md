# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This notice explains how Fenn Agritech uses the personal data it controls. Data your farm keeps in Fenn is covered by the [Data Processing Agreement](data-processing-agreement.md) instead. Your farm decides how that data is used.

## Personal data Fenn keeps

- **Billing records.** The name and email of your farm's billing contact, and your invoices.
- **Support records.** Messages you send Fenn's support team and Fenn's replies.
- **Security logs.** Sign-ins to your Fenn account, including the device and IP address used.
- **Email records.** When Fenn sent an account email, such as an invitation or password reset, who it went to, and whether it arrived.
- **Contact details for notices.** Your contact details, for service and security notices.
- **Incident and complaint records.** Details recorded when Fenn investigates a security incident or handles a data protection complaint.
- **Inquiries.** Your name, contact details, and messages when you contact Fenn to ask about the service or to try it.

Some of this data comes from your farm, such as when someone at your farm adds you as the billing contact.

## Purpose and legal basis

Fenn relies on three legal bases under UK GDPR Article 6(1): contract (b), legal obligation (c), and legitimate interests (f).

- **Billing records:** to charge your farm and keep tax records. Contract, or legitimate interest in billing customers where Fenn's contract is with your business. Keeping invoices: legal obligation (HMRC).
- **Support records:** to answer and follow up on requests. Legitimate interest in supporting customers.
- **Security logs:** to protect Fenn and your account. Legitimate interest in keeping Fenn secure.
- **Email records:** to check account emails arrived. Legitimate interest in running a reliable service.
- **Contact details for notices:** to tell you about the service and incidents. Legitimate interest, or legal obligation where Fenn must notify you.
- **Incident and complaint records:** to investigate incidents and handle complaints. Legal obligation, or legitimate interest in defending legal claims.
- **Inquiries:** to reply. Legitimate interest in responding to inquiries.

Fenn needs your farm's billing details to provide the service. Fenn does not make decisions about people by automated means.

## Who this data is shared with

- **Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland, United States):** processes payments and holds card details.
- **Cloud hosting and email providers (United Kingdom):** store Fenn's records and send account emails.
- **Proton AG (Switzerland):** provides Fenn's email and file storage.
- **Customer support software provider (United Kingdom):** stores support conversations.
- **Providers that send service and security emails:** send notices to customers, including during incidents.
- **Advisers and authorities:** lawyers, forensic investigators, and regulators such as the Information Commission, when Fenn investigates an incident, handles a claim, or the law requires it.

## International data transfers

- **Stripe (Ireland, United States):** the UK recognizes the European Economic Area as providing adequate protection. Stripe, LLC in the US is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the UK also recognizes.
- **Proton (Switzerland):** the UK recognizes Switzerland as providing adequate protection.

## How long data is retained

- **Billing records:** 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to.
- **Support records:** 24 months after the request is closed.
- **Security logs:** 13 months.
- **Email records:** 90 days.
- **Contact details for notices:** until your farm's account is deleted.
- **Incident and complaint records:** 6 years after the incident or complaint is closed.
- **Inquiries:** 24 months after your last contact.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to your data
- Have it corrected or deleted
- Object to or restrict its processing
- Receive it in a portable format (where applicable)

You can object at any time to Fenn using your data for its legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, or to make a complaint, email privacy@fennagritech.io. Fenn acknowledges complaints within 30 days.

You may also complain to the Information Commission ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)), the UK supervisory authority.
